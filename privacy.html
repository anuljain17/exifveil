ExifVeil Privacy Policy

ExifVeil is designed so that inspecting and cleaning a photo does not require sending it to us—or to any app-owned server.

Data we collect

ExifVeil does not collect personal data. The app has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking technology, or third-party SDK that collects information. We do not receive your photos, metadata, usage history, device identifiers, or location.

How photo processing works

Photos you choose are inspected and processed locally on your device. ExifVeil creates new cleaned copies and does not overwrite originals. A copy leaves the app only when you explicitly share it or save it to Photos.

Photos, Files, and iCloud

ExifVeil uses Apple’s system Photos picker and Files picker. If you choose an item stored only in iCloud or another file provider, that system provider may download it under its own privacy terms. ExifVeil does not control or receive that provider traffic.

Local storage and retention

Imported working files, generated private copies, and job receipts can remain inside the app’s private sandbox on your device. Removing the app removes its sandbox. Exported copies remain wherever you chose to save or share them.

Network access

ExifVeil does not operate an application backend and does not make app-owned network requests. System services such as iCloud may use a network connection when you select a cloud-only file.

Children’s privacy

Because ExifVeil does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected on this page and identified by a revised effective date. The current App Store listing will link to the latest policy.

Questions

Visit ExifVeil Support for help and current contact options.
